Run the stream
Legacy Bible Church · Sunday morning

The stream runs from the church laptop in OBS. Everything is already set up. The countdown and the song words come from the laptop by themselves. You check the picture and press one button.

Before the service (5 minutes)

1
On the laptop, Worship Control is open and its two buttons at the top, Open the TV and Open the stream window, have been clicked. The green window says click once to fill the screen; click it once and leave it.Whoever runs the words usually does this. If the green window is open with the countdown on it, you are good.
2
Open OBS. Look at the big preview. You should see the room, with the countdown over it.No room? The camera cable. No countdown? The green window from step 1.
3
Say a word near the shirt mic and watch the Mic meter at the bottom bounce green.
4
Click Start Streaming. Bottom right. That's it.

During the service

CountdownRuns itself. It fades away at 10:30.
SongsRun themselves. The words appear when the laptop puts a slide up and vanish when it blanks.
A videoWhen it starts on the TV, click the Video scene on the left. When it ends, click Service. The mic is quiet on its own during Video.
SermonNothing to do.

After

✓
Click Stop Streaming. YouTube keeps the recording by itself.

If OBS is not on this laptop yet, or something looks wrong, the one-time setup is in OBS-SETUP.md. Still streaming from the iPad with PRISM? That page is here. Questions: Quaid.
