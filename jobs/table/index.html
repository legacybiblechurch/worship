The Table
Legacy Bible Church · your Sunday

You take care of the Lord’s Table. The elements are ready before anyone sits down, they reach every person during the Table, and the table is cleared after. Three steps, all on Sunday.

Sunday morning

1

2

During the service

3
Hand them out during the Lord’s Table. Watch for the pastor’s cue. Go row by row. Clear the table after the service.
