Lyrics on the stream
Legacy Bible Church · one-time setup

One web page goes into PRISM on the iPad. After that it runs itself: a welcome screen before the service, the words during the songs, and the camera the rest of the time. Nothing to touch on a Sunday.

The address

worship.legacybiblechurch.com/stream/

Add it to PRISM (about two minutes)

1
Open PRISM on the iPad. Go to My Studio, tap Widget, then Web.
2
Paste the address into the URL box, after the https:// PRISM already shows. Name it Lyrics. Tap Save.Open this page on the iPad and tap Copy above, so there is nothing to type.
3
Stretch the widget as big as it goes while PRISM's own buttons stay reachable. Then turn on Lock layer so it cannot move by accident.A gap at the edge is fine. The page is see-through there, so the stream never shows it.
✓
Done. PRISM remembers it. On Sunday just open PRISM and go live.

What it does on Sunday

Before 10:30“Service begins shortly” with the logo and a countdown. It fades away by itself at 10:30.
During a songThe words, big, on a dark panel over the picture. They change when the laptop changes the slide. They vanish when the laptop blanks the TV.
Everything elseNothing. The page is see-through and the camera shows.

See it now

This is what the stream shows right now. On a weekday the welcome screen shows for a few seconds, then the camera.

The words and the welcome screen come from the church laptop automatically. If they ever do not show, the laptop with Worship Control is probably not open yet. Questions: Quaid.
