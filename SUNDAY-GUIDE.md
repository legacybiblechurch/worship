# Running worship lyrics — the whole thing on one page

Everything is a website. Nothing to install. Bookmark this:
**https://worship.legacybiblechurch.com**

---

## Before Sunday — build the service (10 minutes)

The whole Sunday is one list, top to bottom: songs, the reading, the sermon, the Lord's Table, a video, the logo. Most weeks the list stays and you change what changed.

1. Open the page above → **Prepare Sunday**.
2. First time on a new computer only: click **Connect this computer** (top right) and follow the six steps. It's a one-time password so the site can save. Never again after that.
3. **Songs:** type a name in the **Add** box.
   - A song we've done before appears in the list — click it. Done.
   - A new song: click **Find "…" on YouTube**. Wait 1–3 minutes. It shows you the videos it found — click **Use this one** on the one that sounds right. If it asks a **Quick check**, listen to the clip and tap the words you hear.
   - Words wrong? Click the song, fix the **Words** box, **Save**.
4. **Reading:** click **Reading**, type the passage ("Psalm 24", "John 3:16-21"), click **Get the verses**. The NASB comes straight in once the church's API.Bible key is on that computer (the Reading box asks for it once; it is on api.bible under the Legacy Bible Church Worship app).
5. **Sermon:** click **Sermon**. Title, passage, and the points one per line. Each point is its own slide.
6. **Lord's Table:** click **Lord's Table**. The words are written once and kept.
7. **Video** or **Logo** are spots in the order: the TV shows the logo there, and Control shows a note about the video.
8. Put everything in order with ▲ ▼. Remove with ✕.
9. Click **Ready for Sunday**. If it complains, it tells you exactly what needs what.

The right pane always shows exactly what the TV will show.

## Sunday morning — running it (2 minutes to set up)

1. On the church laptop, open the page above → **Run the service**.
2. Click **Open display** (top right). Drag that window onto the TV, press **F11** (or the green button) for full screen.
3. Back on Control you'll see a green **Display connected**.

**During the service:**
- **→** or **Next** = next slide.  **←** or **Prev** = back.
- **B** or **Blank** = TV shows the church logo (use between songs, during prayer).
- **▶ Play** next to the song title starts the recording. A small player appears bottom-right.
- Opening song ends → click **Fade out** (or **F**).
- Closing songs → click **Fade into next →** (or **N**): the music crossfades and the slides move to the next song, in one press.

**From a phone instead:** on the phone go to **worship.legacybiblechurch.com/remote** and tap **Connect**. The phone now moves the TV and can Play / Fade the songs on the laptop. Leave Control open on the laptop.

## The livestream

Nothing to do. The lyrics page is already in PRISM on the iPad (set up once from **worship.legacybiblechurch.com/stream/setup/**). Whoever runs the stream opens PRISM and goes live. Before 10:30 the stream shows "Service begins shortly"; when you put a slide up it shows the words; when you blank, it shows the camera.

## If something looks wrong

- **TV shows old songs?** Press **Cmd+Shift+R** on Control and on Display.
- **Display says "not open"?** Click **Open display** again.
- **Closed a window by accident?** Just reopen it — it comes back on the same slide.
- **A word is wrong on the TV?** Fix it in the Studio (Words box → Save). About a minute later, reload Control and Display.
- **Nothing plays when you press Play?** Click anywhere on the Control page once, then press Play again (browsers need one real click before they'll play sound).
